How It Works
Learn how the Peon economy, buildings, and progression work.
Gold
+10g per completed task. Daily cap at 80 tasks (gold mine depletes). Payday bonus every Friday at 5 PM. Fatigue reduces gold income.
Lumber
+5 lumber per session start, +1 per prompt. Lumber Mill doubles all lumber. Click glowing trees on the base map to harvest bonus lumber.
💤 Fatigue
+1 per task completed. At 60: tired (half gold). At 90: exhausted (no gold, combos break). Use peon rest (20 lumber) or click Rest to reset. Tavern raises thresholds by 30.
Buildings
13 structures, each with a unique perk. Click empty tiles on the base map to place. Highlights: Tavern (+30 fatigue threshold), Blacksmith (+50% item effects, halves durability loss), Citadel (2x item drops).
Combos
Consecutive task completions (requires War Mill, caps at 100). Higher combos = better item drops. Broken by exhaustion, context limits, or 1 hour of silence. Altar restores combos once/day.
🛠️ Durability
Equipped items lose 1 durability per 2 tasks. At 0 they break and their effect stops. Use peon repair to fix damaged items. Broken items cost 2x. Blacksmith halves durability loss. Mask of Death halves repair cost.
Repair costs: Common 12g · Uncommon 25g · Rare 50g · Epic 100g · Legendary 250g
🔥 Sass
Peon gets grumpy from overwork (30+ daily tasks), fatigue, gold hoarding, marathon sessions, late nights, and context limits. Burrow suppresses for 1 hour. Frostmourne turns sass into compliments.
🏆 Achievements
17 achievements (3 secret). Track fatigue, task milestones, streaks, repairs, and more. Hover any achievement to see progress.
